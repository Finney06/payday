What if something goes wrong?

Payday is a direct payment, like a bank transfer. Payday never holds the money, so here's what happens when things go wrong.

The client pays too little

Payday checks the payment before it's sent. Too little, or not for this invoice, and it's refused. The client keeps their money.

The price moves

Pay in ParyonUSD and $50 is always $50. Pay in BCH and the amount is locked for 20 minutes.

The wallet is short

The pay page checks first. Not enough dollars but enough BCH? It offers to switch. Empty wallet? It says so, and nothing is sent.

Payday goes down

Your money is already in your own wallet, and every payment is public on the blockchain. You never need Payday to reach your money.

The client wants a refund

Blockchain payments can't be reversed, by anyone. You refund from your own wallet, the way you would with cash or a bank transfer.

The work isn't delivered

Payday doesn't hold money in the middle, so it's the same trust as a normal invoice. Protected payments (escrow) are on our roadmap.
