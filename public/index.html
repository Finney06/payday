From chat to paid in three steps

You never leave the chat. Your client never makes an account.

  1. Chat

    Type one line

    Tell the bot who, how much and what for. Price it in dollars, pounds or naira.

    invoice Tom £40 website fixes
  2. Link

    Share the pay link

    The bot replies with a link and a ready-made message. Send it on WhatsApp, email, anywhere.

    payday/pay/k7m2xq9d

    Get paid in seconds

    Your client scans a QR code with their wallet and pays. The bot tells you the moment it lands.
