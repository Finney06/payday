The Story of Payday

I build websites for clients in Nigeria and the UK. Every job ends the same way: make an invoice, send a PDF, then chase payment on WhatsApp.

My UK clients pay into a foreign account app. Then I convert to naira and lose some to fees.

I'm on WhatsApp and Telegram all day. Why can't I send an invoice like a message, and get paid straight to my own wallet?

Na so Payday start. (That's how Payday started.)

Benefits of Payday

  • ●Invoice in one line
  • ○"invoice Sarah $50 logo design", in $, £ or ₦
  • ●Your client pays in seconds
  • ○Scan a QR with any WizardConnect wallet
  • ●Paid in dollars, not price swings
  • ○ParyonUSD stablecoin, or BCH
  • ●Your money, your wallet
  • ○Non-custodial: Payday never holds funds
  • ●The bot tells you the moment you are paid

BCH Usage

Clients pay in ParyonUSD or BCH, straight to the freelancer's own wallet.

// Every payment carries its invoice id on chain

function opReturnInvoice(invoiceId) {

return Uint8Array.from([0x6a,

...encodeDataPush(utf8ToBin('PAYDAY')),

...encodeDataPush(utf8ToBin(invoiceId))]);

}
